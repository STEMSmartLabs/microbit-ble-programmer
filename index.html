Compatibility and safety notes
  • This tool performs MakeCode programming on micro:bit V2; it does not perform unrestricted full-firmware DFU.
  • A matching runtime is the safe path. When hashes differ, an explicit experimental force option is shown.
  • Forced mismatch programming rewrites only the user-program region and does not replace the installed runtime.
  • Keep the micro:bit powered and the browser tab open until completion.
